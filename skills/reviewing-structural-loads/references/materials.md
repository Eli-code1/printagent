# FDM material reference (approximate)

Figures are nominal and vary widely with print settings, infill, and brand. Use for relative
reasoning, not certification. Density g/cm^3; tensile strength XY in MPa (well-printed,
high-infill); Z-strength factor = fraction of XY strength achieved across layer lines.

| Material | Density | Tensile XY (MPa) | Z factor | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PLA | 1.24 | ~50 | 0.45-0.6 | Stiff, brittle, easy; poor heat resistance |
| PETG | 1.27 | ~50 | 0.5-0.7 | Tougher, slight flex, good layer bonding |
| ABS | 1.04 | ~40 | 0.4-0.55 | Heat resistant, warps; needs enclosure |
| ASA | 1.07 | ~45 | 0.45-0.6 | UV-stable ABS analog |
| PC | 1.20 | ~60 | 0.4-0.6 | Strong, high temp; hard to print |
| Nylon (PA) | 1.14 | ~50 | 0.5-0.7 | Tough, abrasion resistant; hygroscopic |
| TPU (95A) | 1.21 | ~30 | 0.6-0.8 | Flexible; strength is elongation-driven |
| PLA-CF | 1.30 | ~55 | 0.4-0.55 | Stiffer, more brittle, abrasive |
| PETG-CF | 1.30 | ~55 | 0.45-0.6 | Stiff, low-warp |

Density used for mass; Z factor used to scale the anisotropy warning. Orient the principal
tensile load in the XY plane wherever possible.
